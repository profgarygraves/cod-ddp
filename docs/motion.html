Animation

Careers

Editors, motion designers, and animators shape footage, graphics, and characters into finished video for social media, advertising, film, games, and events. The work moves from storyboards to editing, animation, sound, and delivery.

"Typical entry education" is what most people in the job have. Creative employers hire from portfolios, so a COD certificate plus strong work can start your career, and a transfer degree keeps the bachelor's option open. About 1 in 3 film and video editors, and about 3 in 5 animators, are self-employed.

Career ladder

  1. Start

    Production assistant, assistant editor, social video creator

  2. Grow

    Video editor, motion designer, 3D or character animator

  3. Lead

    Senior editor, animation lead, producer or director

Where the jobs are

  • Marketing and social media teams
  • Production companies and post-production houses
  • Film festivals, music festivals, and live events
  • Broadcast and streaming media
  • Game and animation studios
  • Freelance projects and your own channel

Skills employers want

  • Premiere Pro and After Effects
  • Storyboarding and visual storytelling
  • 3D modeling, rigging, and animation
  • Sound, color, and delivery formats
  • Adobe Certified Professional exams
  • A demo reel

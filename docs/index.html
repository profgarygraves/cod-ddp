Turn your creativity into a career.

Designers, editors, animators, photographers, and UX designers start here. Choose a career path, take its first class, and build a portfolio employers want to see.

  • Graphic design & branding

    Graphic designer, brand designer, marketing designer, art director

    $31.68 median for local graphic designers

    Or transfer to a four-year design program with the Design Studies AA.

    Start with DDP 1002 degrees, 2 certificates

  • Video, motion & animation

    Video editor, motion designer, animator, content creator

    $45.88 median for local special effects artists & animators

    Start with DDP 1202 certificates

  • Photography & drones

    Commercial photographer, drone pilot, photo editor

    Start with DDP 1312 certificates

  • Web, UX & games

    UX designer, web designer, front-end developer, game designer

    $34.81 median for local web developers

    Start with DDP 1511 degree, 2 certificates

  • Accessibility

    Accessibility specialist, assistive technology specialist, document remediation specialist

    Start with DDP 2001 certificate

  • Job-ready software skills Free

    Adobe Certified skills in Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, Premiere Pro, and After Effects. Noncredit, no experience needed.

    Start with DDP 301A5 certificates of completion

Get started in four steps

From your first class to a finished certificate. See the details.

  1. Apply to COD

    Admission is free. Already a student? Make a DDP program your major.

  2. Take a start course

    DDP 100, or the first course on your path. It counts toward your award.

  3. Plan with the advisor

    Map your semesters with Mari Abril, DDP program advisor, and a counselor.

  4. Build and apply

    Finish your portfolio in DDP 195, then apply for your certificate or degree.

What creative work pays here

Median pay in the Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario metro, which includes the Coachella Valley. More on careers.

  • $31.68/hrGraphic designers ($65,900 a year)
  • $34.81/hrWeb developers ($72,400 a year)
  • $45.88/hrSpecial effects artists & animators ($95,420 a year), usually with a bachelor's degree

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics, May 2025, Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario metro.

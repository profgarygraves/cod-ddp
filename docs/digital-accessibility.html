Accessibility

Every college, government agency, and large company must make its websites and documents usable by people with disabilities. Accessibility specialists know the law, fix inaccessible content, and match people with assistive technology.

Careers

Accessibility specialist, assistive technology specialist, document remediation specialist

Start with DDP 200

Accessibility law and standards: Section 504, Section 508, ADA, and WCAG. It counts toward the programs on this path, so you can try the field before you commit.

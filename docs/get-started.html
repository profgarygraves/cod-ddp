Get started

From your first class to a finished certificate, in four steps. New to college? You belong here.

  1. Apply to COD

    Apply online through COD Admissions; there is no application fee. If you already take classes at COD, ask Counseling to update your major so your education plan matches your goal. Apply to COD

  2. Take a start course

    Each career path lists one start course. It counts toward that path's certificates and degrees, so nothing is wasted while you decide. Not sure which path? Take DDP 100. Compare the programs

  3. Plan with the advisor

    Meet with Mari Abril, the DDP program advisor, about classes and portfolios, and with a counselor for a full education plan, including general education and transfer. Make a counseling appointment

  4. Build and apply

    Certificates and degrees are not automatic. Apply in the term you finish your last required course. Application for a Degree or Certificate

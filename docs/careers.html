Creative careers

What DDP graduates do, what the work pays in the Inland Empire and Coachella Valley, and how to build experience while you study.

Careers by path

Each career path leads to a set of jobs. Many creative professionals also freelance or run their own studios.

  • Graphic design & brandingGraphic designer, brand designer, marketing designer, art director
  • Video, motion & animationVideo editor, motion designer, animator, content creator
  • Photography & dronesCommercial photographer, drone pilot, photo editor
  • Web, UX & gamesUX designer, web designer, front-end developer, game designer
  • AccessibilityAccessibility specialist, assistive technology specialist, document remediation specialist
  • Job-ready software skillsAdobe Certified skills for any creative or marketing job

Build experience while you study

Employers hire from portfolios. These courses and services help you build one and get it in front of people.

  • DDP 195: Business Practices and Portfolio PreparationFreelancing, pricing, clients, contracts, taxes, and a professional portfolio.
  • DDP 128: Portfolio PreparationOnline portfolio, artist statement, CV, and a public exhibition of student work.
  • DDP 095A: Work ExperienceEarn 1 to 5 units for a design job or internship you already have.
  • Adobe Certified examsTuition-free noncredit classes prepare you for certification in five Adobe apps.
  • Career and Workforce Solutions CenterCareer counseling, resumes, assessments, and job and career fairs.
  • Start your own creative businessPair DDP with COD's Entrepreneurship (ENTR) courses.

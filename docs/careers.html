Build experience while you study

Employers hire from portfolios. These courses and services help you build one and get it in front of people.

  • DDP 195: Business Practices and Portfolio PreparationFreelancing, pricing, clients, contracts, taxes, and a professional portfolio.
  • DDP 128: Portfolio PreparationOnline portfolio, artist statement, CV, and a public exhibition of student work.
  • DDP 095A: Work ExperienceEarn 1 to 5 units for a design job or internship you already have.
  • Find jobs and internships on HandshakeCOD's job board for students and alumni. Sign in with your COD account.
  • Career and Workforce Solutions CenterCareer counseling, resumes, assessments, and job and career fairs.
  • Start your own creative businessPair DDP with COD's Entrepreneurship (ENTR) courses.

Find your path

Six areas, seventeen degrees and certificates. Each path starts with one course that counts toward your award.

  • Graphic design & branding

    Logos, posters, packaging, ads, and type.

    Start with DDP 100. 2 degrees, 2 certificates.

  • Video, motion & animation

    Editing, animated titles, 3D characters, and storytelling.

    Start with DDP 120. 2 certificates.

  • Photography & drone imaging

    Professional images, from the studio to the sky.

    Start with DDP 131. 2 certificates.

  • Web, UX & games

    Websites, apps, and games people enjoy using.

    Start with DDP 151. 1 degree, 2 certificates.

  • Accessibility

    Digital content that works for everyone, and the laws behind it.

    Start with DDP 200. 1 certificate.

  • Learn the software

    Free noncredit Adobe classes. No experience needed.

    Start with DDP 301A. 5 certificates of completion.

Not sure yet? Start with DDP 100.

It samples every part of the field, so you can find what you love before you choose a path.

DDP 100

Introduction to Digital Art & Media. 3 units.

Imaging, illustration, layout, animation, 3D, and new tools. Open to everyone.

  • Transfers to CSU and UC (C-ID ARTS 250)
  • Counts for COD general education, Area A3
  • Required for four DDP degrees and certificates

Learn Adobe for free

Noncredit classes in Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, Premiere Pro, and After Effects cost nothing and need no experience. Finish part A and part B to earn a Certificate of Completion and prepare for the Adobe Certified exam.

Get started in four steps

From your first class to a finished certificate.

  1. Apply to COD

    Admission is free through CCCApply. Already a student? Make a DDP program your major.

  2. Take a start course

    DDP 100, or the first course on your path. It counts toward your award.

  3. Plan with the advisor

    Map your semesters with Mari Abril, DDP program advisor, and a counselor.

  4. Build and apply

    Finish your portfolio in DDP 195, then apply for your certificate or degree.

What creative work pays here

Median pay in the Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario metro, which includes the Coachella Valley.

  • $31.68/hrGraphic designers ($65,900 a year)
  • $34.81/hrWeb developers ($72,400 a year)
  • $45.88/hrSpecial effects artists & animators, usually with a bachelor's degree

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics, May 2025.

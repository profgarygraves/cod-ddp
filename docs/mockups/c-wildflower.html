Get started in four steps

From your first class to a finished certificate.

  1. Apply to COD

    Admission is free through CCCApply. Already a student? Make a DDP program your major.

  2. Take a start course

    DDP 100, or the first course on your path. It counts toward your award.

  3. Plan with the advisor

    Map your semesters with Mari Abril, DDP program advisor, and a counselor.

  4. Build and apply

    Finish your portfolio in DDP 195, then apply for your certificate or degree.

What creative work pays here

Median pay in the Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario metro, which includes the Coachella Valley.

  • $31.68an hour for graphic designers ($65,900 a year)
  • $34.81an hour for web developers ($72,400 a year)
  • $45.88an hour for special effects artists & animators, usually with a bachelor's degree

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics, May 2025.

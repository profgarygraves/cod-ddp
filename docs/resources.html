Student resources

Gear, software, forms, and help for DDP students.

Cameras and gear

Some photography courses ask you to bring your own camera. The catalog lists these requirements.

Camera requirements by course
CourseWhat you need
DDP 130Your own digital SLR (DSLR) camera
DDP 131A digital camera with manual f-stop and shutter speed, and its manual
DDP 132A digital camera (3 megapixels or more); image editing software and a photo printer recommended
DDP 133Your camera and its manual; field trips
DDP 135A 35mm film camera with manual f-stop and shutter speed
DDP 136Drones or drone simulators are provided in class

Software access: ask your instructor how to get Adobe Creative Cloud for your class. (Department to confirm lab and license details.)

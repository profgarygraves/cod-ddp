Accessibility

This site is built so everyone can use it.

The Digital Design & Production website is designed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. Every page is checked with automated tests in light and dark modes, at desktop and phone sizes, and by keyboard.

  • Text and controls meet color contrast standards in both color themes.
  • Every page works with a keyboard, starts with a skip link, and shows where keyboard focus is.
  • Pages reflow on small screens and at 200 percent zoom without side scrolling.
  • Course filters and the color theme switch work without JavaScript.

Found a problem? Report it to College of the Desert or email the program advisor at mabril@collegeofthedesert.edu. For accommodations in classes and labs, contact Disabled Students Programs & Services.

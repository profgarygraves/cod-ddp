Show your work

Your work could appear in the DDP Online Gallery. Here is how to submit a finished piece.

  1. Pick your best piece

    A finished project from any DDP class: poster, photo, video, animation, app, game, or type.

  2. Ask your instructor

    Your instructor can nominate it, or you can submit it yourself.

  3. Sign a release

    We need your written permission before anything is posted. You choose how your name appears.

  4. Send it in

    Email the file, title, course, and a one-line description to Prof. Gary Graves for the DDP Online Gallery.

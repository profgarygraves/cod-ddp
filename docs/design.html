Graphic design & branding

Graphic designers turn ideas into logos, posters, packaging, ads, publications, and social content. You'll learn the design process from research and sketches to finished work, on the Adobe tools studios use, and you'll leave with a portfolio.

Careers: Graphic designer, brand designer, marketing designer, art director

$31.68median hourly pay for graphic designers in the Inland Empire and Coachella Valley

More careers and pay

Start with DDP 100

The open-to-everyone survey: imaging, illustration, layout, animation, 3D, and new tech. C-ID ARTS 250. It counts toward the programs on this path, so you can try the field before you commit.
